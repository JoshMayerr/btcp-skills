# Investment evidence matrix

For each investment criterion show the firm's direct criterion or observable portfolio pattern, management position, customer/ARR evidence, model evidence, GTM/operational evidence, public/external evidence, evidence supporting fit, evidence cutting against fit, confidence, unresolved questions, and investor interpretation required.

Distinguish direct public statements from portfolio patterns inferred by the analyst; cite public sources and access dates. Avoid scores, traffic-light totals, overall fit ratings, and recommendations unless explicitly requested. Surface conflicting evidence, missing comparable periods, selection bias, and business-model/stage differences. Leave the conclusion to the investor.

# Operating and minority-investment model

Build only when inputs support it. Where supported include historicals, management plan, drivers, revenue, gross margin, functional expenses, EBITDA, cash flow/balance, primary/secondary investment, pre/post-money valuation, cap table, security classes, option pool, ownership/dilution, exit EV, net debt/cash, proceeds, MOIC/IRR, cases, sensitivities, and visible checks.

Separate historical sources, management forecast, analyst assumptions, calculations, and outputs. Do not back-solve to desired returns. State unknown security terms rather than silently assuming common-stock economics.

When a template is supplied, reproduce applicable sheet order, layout, formulas, formatting, colors, units, and style; treat all company and deal assumptions as examples and document necessary deviations.

Check cap-table/proceeds totals, pre-money + primary = post-money (subject to disclosed adjustments), sources/uses, cash roll-forward, financial statement ties where available, switches/sensitivities, dated investor cash flows, MOIC/IRR, and formula errors. No hidden plugs or hard-coded calculated outputs. Disclose assumptions, analyst adjustments, missing evidence, and return-driving sensitivities.

# SaaS metrics

Build only supported metrics and define numerator, denominator, timing, population, exclusions, and lineage. Possible outputs include ARR/revenue growth, GRR/NRR/logo retention, ACV, attach, expansion mix, gross margin, CAC/payback/LTV-CAC, magic number, sales efficiency, Rule of 40, burn multiple, pipeline coverage, win rate, sales cycle, rep productivity, implementation time, support burden, module/AI adoption, and cohort contribution margin.

Separate bookings, contracted ARR, live ARR, billed ARR, GAAP revenue, and cash. Match sales-cost timing to outcomes and state cost inclusions. Do not calculate LTV/CAC without explicit retention and margin bases; sensitize assumption-heavy estimates. Prefer stage history over CRM snapshots. Separate mature/ramping reps and relevant segments/channels. Distinguish telemetry use from paid entitlement and revenue.

For each headline metric show management value, reconstructed value, coverage, variance, confidence, definition issues, and source location. Flag partial periods, unstable denominators, cohort maturity, gaps, and definition/system changes.

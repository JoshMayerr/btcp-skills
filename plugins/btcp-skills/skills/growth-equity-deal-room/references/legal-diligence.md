# Legal diligence

Frame all work as diligence support for counsel and the investment team, not legal advice.

Where supported, create a contract exception register covering assignment/change of control, renewal/termination, pricing/ramps, data rights, privacy/security, AI-training restrictions, liability/indemnity, missing signatures/exhibits, and contract-to-billing discrepancies. Preserve contract, parties/entities, effective/renewal dates, source location, concise clause text, exception, business impact, confidence, counsel question, and status. Do not infer enforceability; identify missing documents and conflicting amendments.

For NDA review show issue, counterparty position, preferred position, proposed language, negotiation fallback, priority, source clause, and counsel-review status. Do not call terms market-standard without a cited or user-provided benchmark.

Cross-check the contract index, executed agreements/amendments, billing, CRM, security/privacy registers, and management claims. Limit privileged or sensitive content in broad summaries.

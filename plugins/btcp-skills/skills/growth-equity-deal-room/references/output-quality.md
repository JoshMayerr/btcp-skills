# Output quality

## Word

Use Times New Roman 12 point throughout every Word deliverable, including headings, body text, tables, footnotes, headers, and page numbers. Use black text on a white background. Headings may be distinguished with size, weight, spacing, or simple numbering, but remain Times New Roman and black. Do not use blue headings, colored headers, decorative branding, workflow banners, excessive italics, marketing callouts, generic AI language, fragmented one-line prose, or ornamental color.

Use restrained light-gray table shading only when it materially improves navigation. Every shaded cell must have clearly legible foreground text. Never place black or dark text on dark blue or other dark fills. If any dark fill is required by a supplied template, use white text and verify it visually; otherwise remove the dark fill. Maintain strong text/background contrast in every table cell, heading, chart label, footnote, and callout.

Write detailed investment-team prose. Concision means removing repetition and filler, not removing evidence, methodology, tables, caveats, counterpoints, or analysis. Use tables for genuinely comparative information and use full sentences for interpretation. Add clear disclosure footnotes and precise workbook tab/cell references.

Core workstreams must remain separate Word documents as defined in `SKILL.md`. Do not satisfy the assignment with one omnibus customer diligence packet or a compressed one-page summary. The IC synthesis may summarize and cross-reference but may not replace the detailed IC evidence, public-data, value-creation/hiring, management-triangulation, or legal documents.

## Excel

Follow a supplied template; otherwise use clean investment-banking conventions. Separate inputs/calculations/outputs, store assumptions in dedicated cells, keep formulas visible/auditable, avoid hard-coded calculated outputs, put source URLs in cells/comments, include checks, eliminate formula errors, preserve raw messiness in source tabs, and format only analytical outputs cleanly.

Maintain separate workbooks for (a) customer/ARR/cohort analysis, (b) the minority investment/cap-table model, and (c) quantitative public-data source work. Do not merge them into a single workbook unless the user explicitly requests it. Use the supplied financial-model template only for the minority model's architecture and formatting; do not force customer or public-data work into that model.

Check every formatted cell for readability. Dark fills require white text; light fills require black text. Never deliver black text on dark blue shading. Use enough distinction among inputs, formulas, links, outputs, warnings, and checks to make the workbook auditable, while preserving any explicit user-supplied template convention.

## Final QA

- Trace every quantitative conclusion to an uploaded input or cited public source.
- Verify evidence categories and synthetic/placeholder labels.
- Reconcile headlines to source populations and calculations.
- Check periods, units, currencies, signs, formulas, links, filters, hidden content, print areas, page breaks, and rendered appearance.
- Verify cross-document tab/cell references.
- Confirm the required workstreams were delivered as separate files and that no omnibus file replaced them.
- Confirm the customer/ARR workbook and minority investment/cap-table model are separate.
- Confirm the public-data brief has its own quantitative source workbook.
- Confirm every Word element is Times New Roman 12 point with black text on white or light-gray backgrounds.
- Inspect all shaded Word and Excel cells for foreground/background contrast; reject any black text on dark blue or other dark fills.
- Confirm core documents contain substantive evidence, methodology, counter-evidence, limitations, and open questions; reject superficial summaries presented as completed diligence.
- Keep unresolved questions open and avoid implying an investment judgment.
- Remove company-, sector-, and transaction-specific assumptions inherited from examples/templates.

Render every page of every Word deliverable and representative views of every Excel sheet with the applicable document/spreadsheet workflow before delivery. Inspect typography, clipping, page breaks, table widths, blank pages, fill/font contrast, and legibility at normal viewing size. Correct defects and rerender before delivery. Do not claim completion based only on programmatic generation or file existence.

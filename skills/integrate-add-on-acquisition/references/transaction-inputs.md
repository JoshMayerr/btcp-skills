# Transaction inputs and canonical record

## Source hierarchy

Use the most final, specific, and authoritative evidence available. Apply this default hierarchy unless the transaction documents establish another order:

1. Final executed amendment or closing agreement applicable to the term
2. Final signed purchase, merger, contribution, exchange, subscription, or rollover agreement
3. Final closing statement and funds flow
4. Final approved sources-and-uses, debt, payoff, equity, or capitalization schedule
5. Final target financial statements, trial balance, and management-approved forecast
6. Approved board, sponsor, or management schedule
7. Draft agreement, pre-close model, term sheet, or letter of intent
8. Unsupported narrative or oral instruction

Do not assume that agreement labels such as “final” or “execution” prove execution. Inspect signatures, effective dates, version dates, and closing status. Record any conflict between documents at the same or different authority levels.

## Minimum destination evidence

Identify:

- One authoritative portfolio-company master model
- One authoritative pre-close cap table, whether separate or embedded
- Latest completed actual period and current forecast version
- Existing cash, debt, equity, security-class, and fully diluted balances
- Model conventions for units, signs, currencies, periods, formulas, inputs, and adjustments

If two files could both be the authoritative destination, stop before editing either one.

## Target financial evidence

Use the best available combination of:

- Monthly historical income statement, balance sheet, and cash flow or trial balance
- Closing-period actuals through the accounting effective date
- Budget and current forecast
- Chart of accounts and account definitions
- Entity, segment, location, department, and elimination structure
- Debt, cash, working-capital, tax, and transaction-fee schedules
- Approved EBITDA and pro forma adjustment schedules
- Final opening-balance-sheet or purchase-accounting schedule, when applicable

Record whether each amount is monthly, quarterly, YTD, LTM, point-in-time, reported, forecast, or pro forma. Never derive a monthly flow by subtracting incompatible YTD periods or sum balance-sheet snapshots.

## Canonical transaction record

Store each field with its value, currency or units, effective date, source file, page or sheet, cell or section, document status, mapping or interpretation, and approval status.

### Identity and dates

- Portfolio company, acquired company, seller, and legal entities
- Legal closing date
- Accounting effective date
- Consolidation perimeter
- Transaction structure, such as stock, asset, merger, or contribution

### Purchase-price bridge

- Headline enterprise or transaction value
- Cash, debt, debt-like items, and assumed liabilities
- Working-capital, leakage, earnout, escrow, holdback, and other adjustments
- Equity purchase price
- Seller gross proceeds
- Debt payoff and seller deductions
- Cash proceeds and rollover value

Do not conflate enterprise value, equity value, gross proceeds, net proceeds, and rollover value.

### Sources and uses

- New debt by instrument
- Acquirer cash
- Sponsor, co-investor, seller, or management equity
- Rollover contribution or noncash consideration
- Purchase consideration
- Debt repayment
- Transaction, financing, advisory, and other fees
- Escrow, holdback, balance-sheet funding, and other uses

Record whether rollover is displayed as both a source and a use, netted from cash consideration, or presented outside funds flow. Avoid double counting it.

### Rollover and capitalization

For each holder or holder group, record:

- Gross and eligible proceeds
- Rollover basis, percentage, or fixed amount
- Cash proceeds and deductions
- Security surrendered and security received
- Contribution value, issuance price, exchange ratio, and rounding rule
- Issued units, class, voting rights, and economic rights
- Vesting or forfeiture treatment
- Option, warrant, profits-interest, or incentive-pool treatment

### Financial integration policy

- Historical periods to present on a pro forma basis
- Closing-period treatment
- Post-close actual start date
- Forecast integration date and approved forecast version
- Currency and FX policy
- Mapping, elimination, and allocation policy
- Approved annualization, synergy, or other adjustment policy
- Purchase-accounting scope and supporting schedule

## Evidence conflicts

Prefer the governing source only when the source hierarchy and applicability are clear. Otherwise:

1. Preserve both values and their locations.
2. Quantify the impact on consideration, financing, financial results, units, and ownership.
3. Continue unaffected mappings and controls.
4. Mark the affected output `REVIEW_REQUIRED` until an authorized source resolves the conflict.

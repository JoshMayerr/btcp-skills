# Management triangulation

For each material topic capture speaker, role/context, date, management statement, tone or motivation observation labeled as analyst inference, raw supporting evidence, raw contradicting/limiting evidence, confidence, follow-up, and model/IC implication.

Cite transcript page, timestamp, paragraph, or source location. Preserve qualifiers and distinguish first-hand knowledge, aspiration, estimate, and historical fact. Do not infer deception from tone, inconsistency, or missing recall.

Triangulate against customer/ARR data, financials, CRM, usage, contracts, implementation/support records, public evidence, and prior statements. Reconcile population and timing differences before calling a contradiction. Prioritize questions that could change valuation, ownership, cash need, revenue quality, retention, execution, or legal exposure; phrase unresolved matters as questions.
